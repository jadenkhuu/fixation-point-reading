# FixReading — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-05-16

FixReading does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

- A per-tab on/off flag and intensity ratio in `chrome.storage.session`,
  cleared automatically when Chrome closes.
- A global default-intensity value in `chrome.storage.local`, persisted
  on your device.

All of this data stays on your device. None of it is transmitted off
your machine.

## What the extension does NOT do

- No network requests of any kind.
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking.
- No reading of page content beyond the text you actively select.
- No remote code execution.

## Permissions

- `activeTab` — to inject the formatting logic into the page you've
  chosen to enable it on.
- `scripting` — to perform that injection.
- `storage` — to remember your intensity setting between sessions.

## Contact

For questions or issues, open a GitHub issue on this repository.
