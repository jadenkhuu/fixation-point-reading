fix-reading

Fixation-point reading

About

FixReading bolds the leading letters of each word — your eyes anchor on those prefixes and glide through the rest of the line.

Before

Your eyes jump between anchor points instead of scanning every letter.

After

To use it — turn FixReading on, then drag-select any text on a webpage. Click anywhere outside a highlight to clear them all.

Settings

Intensity controls how many leading letters get bolded in each word.
